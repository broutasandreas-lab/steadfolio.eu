It's for once you're ready to connect real accounts - automatic portfolio syncing, deeper analysis tools (Portfolio Audit, XIRR, advanced simulators), and an unlimited, more capable version of Sofia.

No. Nothing is charged without your explicit action. SteadFolio+ pricing and billing will be clearly presented before you ever subscribe.
