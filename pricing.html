Common questions

What to know

Do I need to pay to learn anything?

No. The Academy, assessments, Paper Trading, the simulators, portfolio tracking, Portfolio Audit, XIRR and Sofia (10 messages a day) are all part of the free product today. Nothing on the "Free today" list requires a subscription.

Can I buy SteadFolio+ now?

No. SteadFolio+ is still being built. There is no way to subscribe or pay at the moment, and no payments are active.

What is SteadFolio+ meant to add?

The plan centres on three themes: automating portfolio upkeep, understanding your portfolio more deeply, and staying on track over time. Details may change as we build it, so we list features only once they work.

How much will SteadFolio+ cost?

Pricing hasn't been announced. We'll publish it here before SteadFolio+ launches.

Will I be charged automatically when SteadFolio+ launches?

No. Nothing is charged without your explicit action, and pricing will be shown clearly before you ever subscribe.
