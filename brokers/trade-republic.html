Broker Profile

Trade Republic

An independent look at Trade Republic's fees, features, and who it tends to suit - not a recommendation.

Fees last verified: August 11, 2026 · Page updated: October 6, 2026

Trade Republic at a glance

  • Built for: automated ETF and stock savings plans from €1, in a mobile-first app.
  • Main cost to watch: the €1 settlement fee on every one-off order. That's 2% of a €50 order but 0.1% of a €1,000 order.
  • Not clearly published: the currency conversion (FX) cost.
  • Regulation: a licensed German bank supervised by BaFin.

Overview

Trade Republic is a German mobile-first broker and bank, built around commission-free trading and automated savings plans. It's one of the most widely used apps for savings-plan investing in Europe, especially among investors who prefer investing small, fixed amounts on a regular schedule.

Stocks & ETFs

Trade Republic offers commission-free access to stocks and ETFs, with the only standard cost being the €1 settlement fee on manual, one-off orders. For investors who mostly buy through recurring savings plans, this fee rarely applies.

Fractional Shares

Trade Republic supports fractional shares, allowing investments starting from €1 - one of the lowest entry points among the brokers covered here.

Savings Plans

Savings plans are core to how Trade Republic is designed to be used - you can automate regular investments into stocks or ETFs from €1, with no settlement fee on those scheduled orders.

Example: savings plan vs one-off orders

Say you want to put €50 a month into a broad ETF. Through a savings plan, Trade Republic charges no settlement fee on each scheduled purchase (as of our last check). Placing the same €50 as a manual one-off order each month would cost €1 each time: 2% of every purchase, or €12 a year. For small, regular amounts, how you buy matters as much as where.

Before relying on this, check that the specific ETF you want is offered as a savings plan, and how often plans can run. New to regular investing? Read What Is Dollar-Cost Averaging?

Currency Conversion

Trade Republic's FX cost was not clearly published in the official sources we checked. For many EU investors this matters less than it sounds: a UCITS ETF bought in euros on a European exchange needs no currency conversion at the broker, even if the fund itself holds US or global companies. The fund's value still moves with exchange rates. FX costs matter more if you buy shares that trade in another currency, so check Trade Republic's current price list before you do.

Country Availability

Our comparison data (verified August 11, 2026) lists Trade Republic as available in Greece and other EU countries. Availability, supported products and tax reporting differ by country and can change, so confirm your country is supported on Trade Republic's site before applying.

Regulation

Trade Republic operates as a licensed bank, Trade Republic Bank GmbH, regulated by Germany's Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin).

How your money is protected

Two different EU safety nets apply to two different things. Cash held as a deposit at an EU bank is covered by a deposit guarantee scheme up to €100,000 per depositor per bank (Directive 2014/49/EU). Securities such as ETFs are held for you and kept separate from the firm's own assets. Investor compensation schemes (Directive 97/9/EC, minimum €20,000) cover certain cases where a firm fails and can't return client assets. Neither protects you from investments falling in value. Check Trade Republic's own terms for where uninvested cash is actually held.

Pros & Limitations

Strengths

  • €1 minimum for savings plans
  • No settlement fee within savings plans
  • Fractional shares supported
  • Regulated bank (BaFin, Germany)

Limitations

  • €1 settlement fee on one-off (non-savings-plan) orders
  • FX fee not clearly published - verify before trading in foreign currencies
  • Narrower product range than multi-asset brokers like IBKR

Who It May Suit

Trade Republic tends to suit investors focused on automated, recurring savings plans into stocks and ETFs, particularly those who want to start with very small monthly amounts and prefer a simple, mobile-first experience.

When an Alternative May Fit Better

Trade Republic isn't the only option for regular ETF investing. Depending on how you invest, these profiles may be worth comparing:

  • Scalable Capital: also a German bank built around free ETF savings plans, with an optional paid PRIME+ plan. Availability in Greece was not confirmed when we checked.
  • Trading 212: €0 commission on stocks and ETFs, a published 0.15% FX fee, and AutoInvest "Pies" for recurring investing.
  • DEGIRO: no savings plans or fractional shares, but €1 ETF orders in its Core Selection on Tradegate. Worth a look if you place larger, less frequent manual orders.
  • Revolut: a monthly allowance of free trades tied to your banking plan, if you already use Revolut and trade occasionally.

Questions to Check Before Opening an Account

  1. Is my country supported, and what tax documents will I receive for it?
  2. Will I mostly invest through savings plans or one-off orders? The fee is different.
  3. Is the exact ETF I want available as a savings plan?
  4. Does the ETF trade in euros, or will my purchases involve currency conversion?
  5. Where is my uninvested cash held, and how is it protected?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Trade Republic charge per trade?

€0 commission plus a €1 external settlement fee on one-off stock and ETF orders. The fee is waived for orders placed within a savings plan.

Is Trade Republic good for ETF savings plans?

Savings plans are central to how Trade Republic works: you can invest from €1 per execution, with no settlement fee on scheduled orders as of our last check. Whether it suits you depends on whether the ETFs you want are offered as savings plans and on your country.

Does Trade Republic offer fractional shares?

Yes, Trade Republic supports fractional shares, and savings plans can start from €1.

Does Trade Republic charge for currency conversion?

Its FX cost was not clearly published in the official sources we checked. Buying euro-traded UCITS ETFs avoids a conversion at the broker; for anything traded in another currency, check Trade Republic's current price list.

Is Trade Republic regulated?

Yes. Trade Republic operates as a licensed bank, Trade Republic Bank GmbH, regulated by Germany's BaFin.
