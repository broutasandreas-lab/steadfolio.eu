Broker Profile

eToro

An independent look at eToro's fees, features, and who it tends to suit - not a recommendation.

Last verified: August 11, 2026

Overview

eToro is a multi-asset broker best known for its social and copy-trading features, alongside standard stock, ETF, and crypto investing. It combines commission-free ETF trading with a platform built around following and copying other investors.

Stocks & ETFs

eToro offers €0 commission on real ETF trades. Stock trading costs are less consistently documented across regions in official sources - some accounts report commission-free real stock trading in certain markets, while others cite a small per-trade fee.

Fractional Shares

eToro supports fractional share purchases, letting you invest a fixed amount rather than buying whole shares.

Savings Plans

eToro's support for automated, recurring savings plans was not clearly confirmed from official sources at the time of writing - verify directly if this matters to you.

Regulation

eToro operates through regulated entities depending on client location, including eToro (Europe) Ltd (CySEC, Cyprus) and eToro (UK) Ltd (FCA).

Pros & Limitations

Strengths

  • €0 commission on real ETFs
  • Fractional shares supported
  • Social/copy trading platform, if that interests you
  • Multi-regulator oversight (CySEC, FCA)

Limitations

  • Stock commission and some fees vary by region and aren't consistently published
  • Savings-plan / auto-invest support not clearly confirmed
  • Social trading focus may not suit investors who prefer a simpler, quieter interface

Who It May Suit

eToro tends to suit investors interested in commission-free ETF investing combined with the option to follow or copy other investors' portfolios - less suited to those who specifically want a straightforward, no-social-features platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does eToro charge commission on ETFs?
No, €0 commission on real ETF trades. Stock commission varies by region - verify for your country.

Does eToro offer fractional shares?
Yes, across its available stocks and ETFs.

Is eToro regulated?
Yes, through entities including eToro (Europe) Ltd (CySEC) and eToro (UK) Ltd (FCA).
