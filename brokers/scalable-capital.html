Broker Profile

Scalable Capital

An independent look at Scalable Capital's fees, features, and who it tends to suit - not a recommendation.

Fees last verified: August 11, 2026 · Page updated: October 6, 2026

Scalable Capital at a glance

  • Built for: automated ETF savings plans, free on every plan from €1 (as of our last check).
  • Main cost to watch: the per-order fee on individual trades on the free plan, or the monthly fee if you choose PRIME+.
  • Availability: not confirmed for residents of Greece in the official sources we checked.
  • Regulation: Scalable Capital Bank GmbH, a German bank supervised by BaFin.

Overview

Scalable Capital is a German broker and bank focused heavily on ETF savings plans, alongside individual stock and ETF trading. Its free ETF savings plans across all account tiers are a core part of its positioning for long-term, automated investors.

Stocks & ETFs

Individual stock and ETF trades cost €0.99 on the free plan, or €0 on the paid PRIME+ plan (€4.99/month) for orders of €250 or more. ETF savings plans are free on every plan tier.

Fractional Shares

Scalable Capital's fractional share support was not clearly confirmed from official sources at the time of writing for individual trades - savings plans generally allow investing a fixed euro amount regardless.

Savings Plans

Free ETF (and stock) savings plans are available from €1, on every account tier, including the free plan - this is one of Scalable Capital's central features.

FREE vs PRIME+: which plan fits?

As of our last check, Scalable Capital had a free plan and a paid PRIME+ plan (about €4.99 a month). Savings plans were free on both. The difference was mainly in individual orders: €0.99 per trade on the free plan, €0 on PRIME+ for orders of €250 or more.

For trading costs alone, a subscription only pays for itself if you place enough qualifying individual orders each month to save more than the fee. If you mainly invest through savings plans, the subscription doesn't lower those costs. PRIME+ includes other features too, so compare it against the way you actually invest.

Check before you decide: broker plans and prices change often. The figures on this page reflect Scalable Capital's official pricing page as checked on August 11, 2026. Confirm current plan names and prices on Scalable Capital's site.

Currency Conversion

Scalable Capital did not publish its FX cost as a simple flat percentage in the sources we checked. The card top-up spreads mentioned on its pricing pages (around 0.69–0.99%) are a different cost from converting currency to buy shares. A UCITS ETF bought in euros on a European exchange needs no conversion at the broker, even if the fund holds global companies. The fund's value still moves with exchange rates.

Country Availability

Scalable Capital is not available in every EU country, and we could not confirm availability for residents of Greece from the official sources we checked. If you live outside its core markets, check the country list on Scalable Capital's site first. If it isn't available to you, the alternatives below may be.

Regulation

Scalable Capital operates through Scalable Capital Bank GmbH, a licensed bank regulated by Germany's BaFin.

How your money is protected

Two different EU safety nets apply to two different things. Cash held as a deposit at an EU bank is covered by a deposit guarantee scheme up to €100,000 per depositor per bank (Directive 2014/49/EU). Securities such as ETFs are held for you and kept separate from the firm's own assets. Investor compensation schemes (Directive 97/9/EC, minimum €20,000) cover certain cases where a firm fails and can't return client assets. Neither protects you from investments falling in value. Check Scalable Capital's own terms for where uninvested cash is actually held.

Limitations

Who It May Suit

Scalable Capital tends to suit investors whose main goal is regular, automated ETF savings plans, particularly those who want €0-fee recurring investing without needing to trade individual stocks frequently.

When an Alternative May Fit Better

If Scalable Capital isn't available in your country, or its plan structure doesn't fit, these profiles cover similar needs:

  • Trade Republic: a similar German bank model with savings plans from €1 and no fee on scheduled orders. One-off orders carry a €1 settlement fee. Our comparison data lists it as available in Greece.
  • Trading 212: €0 commission on stocks and ETFs, a published 0.15% FX fee, and AutoInvest "Pies".
  • XTB: €0 commission up to €100,000 of monthly turnover, with automatic Investment Plans.
  • Lightyear: €0 commission on ETF trades, but no savings plans or auto-invest.
