Broker Profile

DEGIRO

An independent look at DEGIRO's fees, features, and who it tends to suit - not a recommendation.

Last verified: August 11, 2026

Overview

DEGIRO is a Netherlands-based broker known for low-cost access to stocks and ETFs across more than 50 exchanges worldwide. It restructured its fee model in October 2025 around a "Core Selection" of ETFs available at a low flat handling fee on the Tradegate exchange.

Fees

Stock commission€2-4.90 per trade, varies by exchange
ETF commission (Core Selection, Tradegate)€1 handling fee
ETF commission (other exchanges)€3 per trade
FX fee0.25%
Custody / inactivity fee€0
Connectivity fee€2.50/year per exchange (non-Tradegate ETFs)
Worth noting: The connectivity fee is waived on your home exchange and on Core Selection ETFs traded via Tradegate. It applies per exchange, once per year, only if you hold ETFs outside the Core Selection.

Stocks & ETFs

DEGIRO offers access to stocks and ETFs across a very wide range of international exchanges. Its Core Selection includes over 1,000 ETFs tradable on Tradegate at a flat €1 handling fee, making it one of the lower-cost options for ETF-focused, buy-and-hold investors who stick to that selection.

Fractional Shares

DEGIRO does not offer fractional shares. Every purchase is for a whole share or ETF unit, which can matter if you're investing small, fixed amounts each month into a higher-priced stock or fund.

Savings Plans

DEGIRO does not currently offer an automated savings plan or auto-invest feature - trades are placed manually.

Regulation

DEGIRO is regulated by the Dutch Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM) and De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB). It operates through flatexDEGIRO Bank AG, which is supervised by Germany's BaFin.

Pros & Limitations

Strengths

  • €1 ETFs within the Core Selection (Tradegate)
  • Access to 50+ international exchanges
  • No custody or inactivity fees
  • Regulated bank entity (flatexDEGIRO Bank AG)

Limitations

  • No fractional shares
  • No savings plan / automatic investing
  • ETFs outside the Core Selection cost more (€3 + possible connectivity fee)
  • Stock commissions vary meaningfully by exchange

Who It May Suit

DEGIRO tends to suit investors who want broad international market access and are comfortable buying whole shares/units, particularly those focused on Core Selection ETFs where the per-trade cost is low. It may suit less well those who want to invest small, fixed euro amounts automatically each month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does DEGIRO charge for ETFs?
Core Selection ETFs on Tradegate cost a €1 handling fee. ETFs outside the Core Selection cost €3 per transaction, potentially plus an annual €2.50 connectivity fee per exchange.

Does DEGIRO offer fractional shares?
No. Only whole shares or ETF units can be purchased.

Is DEGIRO regulated?
Yes, by the Dutch AFM and DNB, operating through flatexDEGIRO Bank AG (supervised by BaFin).
