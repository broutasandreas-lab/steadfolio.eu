Broker Profile

Revolut

An independent look at Revolut's stock trading fees, features, and who it tends to suit - not a recommendation.

Trading fees and plan prices (Greece) verified: October 6, 2026 · Other details last verified: August 11, 2026

Revolut at a glance

  • Built for: existing Revolut users, especially on Premium, Metal or Ultra, who want occasional stock or ETF trades inside the app they already use.
  • Main cost to watch: €1 per executed order beyond your plan's monthly allowance. Standard and Plus include no commission-free orders, so every order is charged, unless it's an eligible ETP purchase made through an investment plan.
  • Recurring investing: investment plans are available; eligible ETP purchases placed through them don't pay the commission.
  • Regulation: investment services are provided by Revolut Securities Europe UAB, regulated by the Bank of Lithuania. Revolut's banking services are a separate entity, Revolut Bank UAB.

Overview

Revolut is best known as a digital banking app, and has added stock and ETF trading as one of its features. Trading costs depend on your account plan: higher plans include a monthly allowance of commission-free orders, and orders beyond it are charged a commission.

Stocks & ETFs

Revolut provides access to a range of stocks and ETFs. Premium, Metal and Ultra include a monthly allowance of commission-free orders. Orders on Standard and Plus, and orders beyond the allowance on higher plans, cost €1 per executed order.

Fractional Shares

Revolut supports fractional share investing, letting you invest a set amount rather than a whole share.

Investment Plans (Recurring Investing)

Revolut offers investment plans for recurring or one-off purchases. Purchases of eligible ETPs (exchange-traded products, which include many ETFs) placed through an investment plan don't pay the €1 commission. Not every ETF or stock is eligible, so check whether the fund you want is included before relying on this.

Plans and the Free-Trade Allowance

Revolut's investing costs depend on your plan, not on a single price list. In Greece:

  • Standard (free) and Plus (€3.49/month): no commission-free orders.
  • Premium (€10.99/month): 5 commission-free orders a month.
  • Metal (€17.99/month) and Ultra (€60/month, introductory price): 10 commission-free orders a month.

Beyond the allowance, each executed order costs €1. Eligible ETP purchases through investment plans are the exception.

Example: what the €1 commission means for small orders

On Standard or Plus, a €100 order costs €1 in commission: 1% of the amount invested. A €2,000 order also costs €1, which is 0.05%. If you invest €100 a month in single orders, that's €12 a year. Routing the same purchases through an investment plan into an eligible ETP would avoid that commission.

Upgrading doesn't pay for itself on trading costs alone. Premium's 5 commission-free orders save at most €5 a month against its €10.99 price, and Metal's 10 save at most €10 against €17.99. Paid plans include other benefits, so choose a plan for those, not for the trading allowance.

Currency Conversion

Many stocks and ETFs on Revolut are priced in US dollars, so converting euros can add cost. According to Revolut's Greek help centre, what you pay depends on your plan, how much you've already exchanged that month (a fair-usage limit on lower plans) and whether you exchange at the weekend. It also depends on where the exchange happens: in the Invest app, or in your main Revolut account before you move the money to investing. We don't quote a single percentage here because the cost differs by plan and situation. Check the current terms for your plan, and consider euro-traded ETFs, which need no conversion.

Country Availability

Our comparison data (verified August 11, 2026) lists Revolut's investing product as available in Greece and other EEA countries. The range of stocks and ETFs offered can differ by country, so check what's available in your app before planning around a specific fund.

Regulation

Revolut's investment services in the European Economic Area are provided by Revolut Securities Europe UAB, an investment firm regulated by the Bank of Lithuania. This is a separate legal entity from Revolut Bank UAB, which provides Revolut's banking services. The difference matters for protection: your investments sit with the investment firm, while cash deposits sit with the bank.

How your money is protected

Two different EU safety nets apply to two different things. Cash held as a deposit at an EU bank is covered by a deposit guarantee scheme up to €100,000 per depositor per bank (Directive 2014/49/EU). Securities such as ETFs are held for you and kept separate from the firm's own assets. Investor compensation schemes (Directive 97/9/EC, minimum €20,000) cover certain cases where a firm fails and can't return client assets. Neither protects you from investments falling in value. Check Revolut's own terms to see which entity holds your investments and where uninvested cash is held.

Pros & Limitations

Strengths

  • Commission-free monthly orders on Premium (5), Metal and Ultra (10)
  • No commission on eligible ETP purchases through investment plans
  • No inactivity or withdrawal fee
  • Fractional shares supported
  • Integrated with an app many users already have for banking

Limitations

  • No commission-free orders on Standard or Plus: €1 per executed order
  • The allowance requires a paid plan (from €10.99/month for Premium)
  • Currency exchange costs depend on your plan and when you exchange
  • Not every ETF is eligible for commission-free investment plans

Who It May Suit

Revolut tends to suit existing Revolut users who either place a few trades a month within a Premium, Metal or Ultra allowance, or invest regularly through investment plans in eligible ETPs. On Standard or Plus, orders outside investment plans cost €1 each.

When an Alternative May Fit Better

If you want to invest a fixed amount every month, or trade more than a few times a month, a dedicated broker may fit better:

  • Trade Republic: savings plans from €1 with no fee on scheduled orders; a €1 settlement fee on one-off orders.
  • Trading 212: €0 commission on stocks and ETFs, a published 0.15% FX fee, and AutoInvest "Pies".
  • XTB: €0 commission up to €100,000 of monthly turnover, with automatic Investment Plans.
  • Scalable Capital: free ETF savings plans on every plan, if it's available in your country.

Questions to Check Before Opening an Account

  1. How many commission-free orders does my plan include, and how many orders would I place in a typical month?
  2. Is the ETF I want eligible for commission-free investment plans?
  3. Does the ETF trade in euros, or will each purchase involve currency exchange? What does my plan charge for that?
  4. Is a paid plan worth its monthly price for the way I invest?
  5. Which Revolut entity holds my investments, and how are they protected?

Frequently Asked Questions

How many commission-free trades does Revolut offer per month?

It depends on your plan. In Greece, Standard and Plus include no commission-free orders, Premium includes 5 a month, and Metal and Ultra include 10, on a rolling monthly basis.

What does Revolut charge per trade beyond the allowance?

In Greece, €1 per executed order. Eligible ETP purchases placed through Revolut's investment plan feature don't pay this commission.

How much do Revolut's plans cost?

In Greece, Standard is free, Plus is €3.49 a month, Premium €10.99, Metal €17.99 and Ultra €60. Revolut shows the Ultra price as an introductory price, so it may change.

Is Revolut good for long-term ETF investing?

It can be, if the ETF you want is eligible for investment plans: eligible ETP purchases through an investment plan don't pay the €1 commission. Otherwise, on Standard or Plus every order costs €1, which weighs heavily on small monthly amounts.

What does Revolut charge for currency exchange when investing?

It depends on your plan, how much you've already exchanged that month, whether it's the weekend, and whether you exchange in the Invest app or your main account. Check Revolut's Greek help centre for your plan's current terms.

Does Revolut offer fractional shares?

Yes, Revolut supports fractional share investing.

Which Revolut company provides investment services in the EEA?

Revolut Securities Europe UAB, an investment firm regulated by the Bank of Lithuania. Revolut's banking services are provided by a separate entity, Revolut Bank UAB.
