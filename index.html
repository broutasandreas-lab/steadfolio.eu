The honest part

Most people don't avoid investing because it's hard.
They avoid it because it feels unsafe to start.

"I don't know where to even begin."

"I'm scared I'll lose money doing this wrong."

"Everyone talks like I should already know this."

Why bother at all

People don't actually want a portfolio.
They want what it leads to.

Keeping up with inflation

Money sitting still quietly loses purchasing power every year. Investing is how it has a chance to keep up.

Building real options later

A home, a career change, retiring on your own terms - long-term investing is what makes those choices possible.

Fewer money-related worries

Understanding what you're doing - and why - replaces vague financial anxiety with an actual plan.
