Quick answers

The questions everyone asks first

Can I start with €50?

Yes - most brokers allow fractional shares today, so there's no real minimum that locks you out.

Can I lose money?

Yes, values can go up and down - which is exactly why SteadFolio starts with education before real money is involved.

Is investing better than a savings account?

Over the long term, investing gives your money a better chance to outpace inflation - though it comes with more short-term ups and downs.

Do I need to know what an ETF is first?

No - Sofia explains it in plain language the moment it's relevant, right when you need it.
